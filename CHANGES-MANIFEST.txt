   MainWindow.xaml.cs)

   "(combat estimate unavailable)" line instead of letting the whole
   Damage/Scripting/Lair section vanish silently. (MonsterVerbose.cs)
3. +2 tests (858/858 total): MonsterDetailText_FlattensFullDossier and
   FlattenDossier_ShapesHeadersDetailsAndSpacers. (Wave18AttackPanelTests.cs)
4. PORT_LOG.md / FEATURE_CENSUS.md updated.
